1. DB-IP City Lite and ASN Lite
-------------------------------

IP Geolocation by DB-IP: https://db-ip.com

The route map takes the places and networks of routers from the IP to City Lite and IP to ASN Lite
databases made by DB-IP.com. The app's downloads (the Windows installer and portable zip, the Linux
and macOS packages) carry them in the geo folder (dbip-city-lite.mmdb, dbip-asn-lite.mmdb);
geo/manifest.json and geo/NOTICE.txt there name their release month. When the user agrees, the
app downloads a newer month from https://download.db-ip.com/free/ into the geo-update folder in its
data folder.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The database files are not covered by the license of Whilo Trace: they are under CC BY 4.0, not under
MIT or Apache-2.0. They are not modified apart from decompression (DB-IP publishes them as .mmdb.gz).
As section 5 of CC BY 4.0 says, they come as-is, without warranties.


2. Natural Earth
----------------

The land outline of the map (app/ui/vendor/land-110m.js: Natural Earth 1:110m land, the land-110m file
of world-atlas 2.0.2) and the country label points (app/ui/vendor/countries.js: Natural Earth 1:10m
admin-0 countries v5.1.2, made by tools/ne_countries.py) are Natural Earth data, which is in the public
domain. Made with Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com
